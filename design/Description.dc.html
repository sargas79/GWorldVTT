Biography

Third daughter of a jarl with nothing left to divide, Brenna took her father's ring and a borrowed sword south at nineteen. Ten years of other people's wars have made her good at exactly one thing, and she has stopped pretending otherwise.

She fights for the Northmarch garrison now — steady pay, and nobody asks what she did in Vantre.
